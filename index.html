SafeVolume SAM Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

SafeVolume SAM ("the app") is published by HappySam StudioQ ("we", "us"). It lets a parent set a maximum media volume on an Android device and keeps the volume from going above it. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: we do not collect your data, we do not share it, and the app has no ads or analytics.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following only on the device where it is installed. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Your volume limit and app settings.
  • Your parent PIN, stored only as a protected hash, never as the PIN itself, plus the count of wrong attempts used to apply a lockout.
  • An activity log of times the volume went above the limit and was lowered, and times protection was interrupted.

Uninstalling the app removes this information. We have no copy of it and cannot recover it.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, and no advertising. We do not know who uses the app or how.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time unlock are sold through Google Play. Google handles payment and tells the app whether you own a purchase. We never see your payment details. Google's handling of that information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. This is the only reason the app uses the internet permission.

Permissions and why the app uses them

  • Accessibility service (optional helper): used only to notice when a volume button is pressed so the limit can be applied right away. It does not read the screen, other apps' content, or anything you type, and it does not record or transmit anything.
  • Foreground service and notification: keeps protection running in the background and shows a persistent notification while it runs.
  • Run at startup: restarts protection after the device restarts.
  • Internet and network state: used by Google Play Billing to confirm your purchase.

Children

The app is a tool for parents and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
